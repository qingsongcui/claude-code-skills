# Q3 2026 Quarterly Business Review — Beaconloop (fictional demo brief)

Fictional company for engine validation only. Beaconloop is a 40-seat B2B SaaS
selling async video standups to remote product teams. All numbers are invented.

## Audience

Board + leadership team. 30-minute read, metrics-first, no jargon walls.

## Goal

Show Q3 2026 performance vs plan, explain the two misses (mid-market churn,
enterprise pipeline slip), and land the Q4 resourcing ask.

## Constraints

- 10 pages, 16:9 (1280x720), master: `investment-blue-light`
- Every content slide must carry real, selectable text (no flattened copy)
- One revenue-bridge chart, captured as a 3x PNG and embedded in the PPTX
- Headline numbers (invented): ARR $4.8M (+31% YoY), NRR 112%, gross churn 3.1%,
  headcount 40, runway 22 months, Q4 ask: 3 hires (2 eng, 1 design)

## Page plan (10)

1. Cover — Q3 2026 Business Review, Beaconloop, metrics tagline
2. Agenda — timeline of the 30-minute flow
3. KPI snapshot — metrics-block: ARR, NRR, churn, runway vs plan
4. Revenue bridge — grid-card + bar chart (Q2 ARR → expansion → new → churn → Q3 ARR)
